What survives the checker?

A green law means every displayed finite case passed. For the natural-number sample, closure failures are artifacts of displaying only 0 through 4; the missing additive inverses are the real obstruction to being a ring. Labels such as “Noetherian” are mathematical facts about the named model, not claims made by this tiny checker.
